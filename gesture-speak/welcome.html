Silence turns a drawing made in the air into a spoken sentence, and turns the other person's reply into text you can read. For people who cannot speak and the people they talk to.

Built in one day at VexoHacks with the Vexo band, Deepgram, OpenAI and ElevenLabs.

You draw it. Silence says it. They answer, and you read every word.

Typing turns a conversation into waiting

When you cannot speak, the usual answer is a phone. You type, the other person stands there, you show them the screen, and the moment is gone. Short questions become long pauses, and it is easier for both sides to stop talking.

Silence keeps both people in it

Their words appear as they speak them. Your answer is a quick drawing, and it is said out loud as a full sentence a second later. Nobody passes a phone back and forth, and nobody waits.

What you get

A sentence, not a single word

Draw a cup after someone asks what you would like, and Silence says "I would like a coffee, please." It reads your drawing together with what was just said.

Their words in large type

What the other person says shows on the screen while they are still talking, about a second behind their voice in our tests.

Quiet when it is unsure

A drawing it cannot read is not turned into a guess. In our test a scribble produced no speech at all. Nothing wrong is said in your name.

Your camera stays yours

The camera picture never leaves your laptop and is never shown. Only the lines you draw are used.

How it works

  1. 1

    They speak

    The microphone in the Vexo band hears the other person, and their words appear on your screen.

  2. 2

    You draw

    Make a fist in front of the camera and draw in the air. Open your hand when you are done.

  3. 3

    Silence speaks

    The drawing becomes the sentence you mean, and it is said out loud in a natural voice.

Questions

Does it understand sign language?

No. Silence reads drawings made in the air, a few still hand shapes such as a thumbs up, and wrist shakes. It is not a sign language translator.

What do I need to use it?

A laptop with a camera, and Chrome or Edge. The Vexo band adds its microphone and wrist shakes. Without the band, the laptop microphone hears the other person.

Is my camera recorded?

No. The hand is found inside the page on your own laptop. The picture is never shown, saved or sent anywhere.

What leaves my laptop?

The other person's speech goes to Deepgram to become text. Your line drawing and the recent conversation go to OpenAI to become a sentence. The sentence goes to ElevenLabs to become a voice.

What if it says the wrong thing?

When a drawing is unclear it says nothing. You can also switch on a step that shows you the sentence first and waits for your yes before it speaks.

Which languages does it work in?

English for now, for both the captions and the spoken sentences.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to install and nothing to sign up for. It opens in your browser.
